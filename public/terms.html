The Vamplus Toronto Restaurant Grader (the "Service") is operated by Vamplus (ahmedfayazwahab.ca). You can reach us through the contact details on the site above.

2. What the Service does

You pick a restaurant from a list of Toronto food premises. The Service then looks at public information about it (its Google Maps profile, its website, web search results and nearby competitors) and produces a score, comparisons, an estimate of revenue that may be at risk, and AI-written summaries and suggestions.

3. Estimates, opinions and no advice

  • Scores and grades come from our own scoring method. Another reasonable method could give different results.
  • Dollar figures are rough estimates built from public review counts and general industry assumptions (shown in each report). They are not measurements of any restaurant's actual sales or losses.
  • AI-written text can be wrong or incomplete. Check it before you act on it.
  • Nothing here is professional, financial, legal, marketing or investment advice.
  • Reports reflect public information at the time of the scan. Information can be wrong, missing or out of date, and some websites block our checks. When that happens, the report says the score is incomplete.
